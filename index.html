Deep Domain Expertise

The Bottleneck of
Outsourced KYC

Currently, Benepay relies on manual 3rd-party outsourcing to conduct KYC and entity verification. While this externalizes the labor, it creates a massive black box: you surrender control over the client onboarding experience, incur high marginal costs per application, and face unpredictable delays when resolving complex Ultimate Beneficial Owners (UBOs).

The OpEx Drag of Manual Verification

Every time an outsourced analyst manually cross-references FATF Grey Lists or extracts data from complex Trust Deeds, your Time-to-Revenue is delayed. Relying on external human dependencies for baseline compliance destroys operational unit economics as transaction volumes scale.

The Futuristic Solution

Supervisor-Worker Agent Swarm

Benepay transcends outsourced manual labor by implementing a probabilistic Agentic architecture. We deploy a synthetic, in-house compliance team where specialized LLMs autonomously gather unstructured data, debate risk profiles, and pre-fill compliance requirements—returning speed and operational leverage directly to your institution.

01
Agentic Auto-Discovery

The client simply provides a Registration Number. The Registry Agent instantly crawls Companies House and Global LEI registers, while the Vision Agent parses complex Trust Deeds to establish a verified "Golden Record" of ownership.

02
Algorithmic Gap Analysis

Rather than presenting clients with a monolithic 50-field form, the Supervisor Agent maps the discovered entity data against strict regulatory schemas. It intelligently highlights only the missing mandatory fields (e.g., specific Sources of Wealth), creating a frictionless, curated user experience.

03
Probabilistic Veracity Checks

The Risk Agent utilizes RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) against FATF profiles in real-time, instantly flagging synthetic identities, high-risk operational geographies, or logically inconsistent inputs before the dossier even reaches human review.
